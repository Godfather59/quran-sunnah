QURAN TRANSLATIONS
Saheeh International and Muhammad Hamidullah translations are sourced via
Tanzil. Tanzil's translation page states downloaded translations are for
non-commercial purposes unless the necessary permission is obtained from the
translator or publisher.
Source: https://tanzil.net/trans/

TAJWEED
Annotations are generated from the cpfair/quran-tajweed decision-tree/data
project. Its Tajweed data file is licensed CC BY 4.0.
Source: https://github.com/cpfair/quran-tajweed

MORPHOLOGY
Quranic Arabic Corpus v0.4, Kais Dukes. GNU GPL plus Corpus terms. Source must
be credited and linked.
Source: https://corpus.quran.com

AL-SIRAJ
Provenance points to QuranEnc. QuranEnc allows republication subject to its
no-modification, attribution, version/transcript, and update conditions. The
exact bundled version identifier still needs to be recorded.
Source: https://quranenc.com

WARSH / QALUN / INDOPAK
Aggregator provenance is recorded, but direct upstream redistribution terms
for the underlying QuranComplex-derived editions remain unresolved.

HADITH
The hadith-api repository is Unlicensed as software/repository material, but
the license/source of its underlying Arabic Hadith edition texts is not clearly
established. Upstream issue #129 asks this exact question and remains open.
Source: https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api/issues/129

FONTS
Amiri Quran and Noto Naskh Arabic are bundled under SIL Open Font License
terms. Their OFL license files are included in assets/fonts/.

See docs/DATA_LICENSES.md in the source repository for the detailed audit.
