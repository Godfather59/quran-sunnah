Word morphology (Hafs) — per-surah JSON
==========================================
Source    : Quranic Arabic Corpus v0.4 (Kais Dukes, Leeds) via the
            mustafa0x/quran-morphology fork (Buckwalter→Arabic,
            reproducible from the original by script).
License   : GNU GPL with Terms of Use — app use explicitly permitted
            provided the source is credited and linked:
            http://corpus.quran.com (credited in-app in Word Meanings).
Generated : 2026-10-04 by aligning corpus segments to OUR EXACT
            bundled Tanzil text (token-level, normalized comparison).
Coverage  : 82,260 tokens; 98.6% carry lemma/root/POS; 286 tokens
            (0.35%) show the word with no gloss rather than a guess.
            Pause marks tagged MARK; basmala prefixes glossed from 1:1.
Layout    : words/hafs/<surah>.json {entries: [{ayah, words:
            [{w, lemma, root, pos}]}]}.
Note      : lemmas/roots are dictionary forms for study, not full
            translations — pair with the ayah translation + tafsir.
